Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HK JINXIN DEVELOPMENT LIMITED (香港錦鑫發展有限公司, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you use our mobile applications, games and related services (the “Apps”) and our website at hkjixin.com. It applies to all Apps published under our developer accounts on Google Play and the App Store, unless an App provides its own privacy policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. Our Apps generally do not require you to create an account. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Content on your device. Some Apps may request access to your camera, photos, files or microphone in order to provide their core features. This content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers unless a feature clearly states otherwise and you choose to use it.

Information collected automatically. We and our service providers may collect device information (such as device model, operating system version and language settings), advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, in-app activity, and crash and performance diagnostics.

2. Third-party services

Our Apps may use third-party services that collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google Play Services — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Privacy & messaging
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics — Privacy information
  • Meta Audience Network — Privacy Policy
  • Pangle — Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral — Privacy Policy
  • Adjust — Privacy Policy

The specific services used may differ between Apps. Each App’s data safety information on Google Play describes what that App collects.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, maintain and improve the Apps;
  • To display advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted;
  • To measure App performance and the effectiveness of our marketing;
  • To detect, diagnose and fix technical issues;
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Advertising and your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before using your data for personalized advertising.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers described above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you by contacting us at contact@hkjixin.com. Uninstalling an App removes data stored locally by that App on your device.

7. Children’s privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards provided by those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
