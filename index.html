About us

A focused team building the next generation of everyday apps.

Founded in Hong Kong in 2024, HK JINXIN DEVELOPMENT LIMITED (香港錦鑫發展有限公司) is a technology company dedicated to the design, development and global publishing of mobile applications on Android and iOS.

We combine product craftsmanship with data-driven growth: every app we build starts from a real user need, is shaped by careful design, and is improved continuously through measurement and feedback.

Our goal is straightforward — to create reliable, high-quality apps that people around the world enjoy using every day.

Our principles

What guides our work.

These commitments shape every decision we make, from the first line of code to the way we support our users.

Privacy by design

We collect only what an app genuinely needs, are transparent about it, and follow Google Play and App Store policies.

Quality first

Fast, stable and intuitive apps — tested thoroughly on real devices before they reach our users.

Data-informed decisions

We let evidence, not assumptions, guide what we build next and how we improve it.

Responsive support

Every user message matters. We respond to feedback and support requests promptly and respectfully.
